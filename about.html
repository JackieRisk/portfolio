Jackie Risk

Jackie is a Senior Product Designer with 10 years of experience leading end-to-end UX across SaaS, enterprise, and federal platforms.

At Workleap, she co-led a principal-level redesign of the platform's information architecture and navigation, integrating AI-assisted workflows and improving onboarding completion from 43% to 85% through continuous discovery and A/B experimentation.

Previously, as Principal Product Designer at EAGLE6, she spent six years building Section 508 / WCAG-compliant design systems for a cybersecurity platform used by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

She works best in async, remote-first teams where design has a real seat at the table, collaborating with product and engineering from problem framing through launch.
